# Code of Conduct

IpeSign contributors and maintainers are expected to provide a welcoming, harassment-free environment regardless of experience, identity, background or viewpoint.

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly, and keep technical disagreements focused on evidence and project goals. Harassment, threats, discrimination, publication of private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer at `salmoria.dev@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
